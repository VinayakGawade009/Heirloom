My dear Mohan,

The monsoon rains arrived late this season, and the harvest at our Sultanpur fields yielded far less than we had prayed for. Your uncle mentioned that the stone well requires immediate repair before the winter chill sets in, and the mortar along the eastern boundary furrow is already giving way. We received word that the sowing of the autumn grain in the lower valley was delayed until mid-July. Keep well in your studies and write to us as soon as you arrive in Lucknow.

— Yours affectionately, Nani

Translator’s Note (Local Model)

The term “eastern furrow” corresponds to purva kholi in the original regional idiom, denoting the family irrigation plot established in 1934.
